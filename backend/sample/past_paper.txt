Biology Past Paper (sample)

1. Explain what happens in the light reaction of photosynthesis. Where does it take place?
2. Name the enzyme that fixes carbon dioxide in the Calvin cycle.
3. State two factors that limit the rate of photosynthesis.
4. Describe the Calvin cycle and state why it needs the products of the light reaction.
5. Compare aerobic and anaerobic respiration in terms of ATP yield.
6. Where does glycolysis occur and what are its products?
7. Differentiate between mitosis and meiosis.
8. Explain how crossing over creates genetic variation.
